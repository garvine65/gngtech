Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is G&G Tech based and who do you serve? +

G&G Tech is proudly based in Kenya 🇰🇪. We serve local startups, growing SMEs, and international businesses looking for modern, high-performance websites, mobile apps, and custom software systems.

How long does a typical project take to complete? +

Standard business websites are typically delivered in 1 to 2 weeks. Web applications, mobile apps, and specialized business management systems generally take 2 to 4 weeks depending on overall features and scope.

Can you integrate local platforms like M-Pesa or SMS gateways? +

Yes! We specialize in custom integrations including M-Pesa (Daraja API), bank payment gateways, SMS notification APIs (such as Africa's Talking), automated invoicing, and custom database APIs.

Do you offer maintenance and technical support after launch? +

Yes. We provide ongoing technical support, cloud hosting configuration, security monitoring, performance tuning, and feature improvements so your platform remains fast, secure, and up to date.

How do project payments and consultations work? +

We begin with a free consultation to review your requirements. Projects follow transparent milestone payments (initial deposit, progress demo review, and final handover) so you maintain 100% clarity.
